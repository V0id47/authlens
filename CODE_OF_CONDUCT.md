# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of private information and publication of others' secrets are not acceptable.

Use synthetic examples in public discussions. Assume different experience levels and language backgrounds. Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, warn participants or restrict participation, considering severity and context. Apply these rules consistently and explain moderation decisions when privacy permits.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through the hosting platform. Until a private channel is established, request one without posting identifying details publicly. This project does not promise a staffed incident-response service.
